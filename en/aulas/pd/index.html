Lesson 2 · 16 min

The bottom layer: PD control at the joints

Between the policy and the motor sits a PD controller on each joint, with kp and kd chosen during training. In this lesson you measure a knee's response to a step with three kd values, hold the G1 upright with PD alone, and find out why the policy's gains can't keep the robot standing on their own.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Torque = kp × (target − position) − kd × velocity, limited by the motor
  2. 249% overshoot with no kd; 1% with kd 2
  3. 3The per-joint gains in deploy.yaml (knee 150/4, ankle 40/2)
  4. 4Stiff PD holds the G1 up; the policy's gains alone don't

Goal: measure the effect of kp and kd on a joint and hold the G1 upright with PD alone, understanding why the policy's gains aren't enough without the policy.

02 · One knee, three brakes

degrau_junta.py frees the joint from the pelvis (the G1 hangs in the air) and sends the left knee from 0.3 to 0.8 rad with kp 100 and kd 0, 2, and 10.

Terminal · step on the knee
F=${FTH:-$HOME/fth}; cd $F/locomocao && . $F/.venv/bin/activate
for kd in 0 2 10; do python3 kit/degrau_junta.py --kp 100 --kd $kd | tail -2; done
Example from the repository
sobressinal: 49 % · posiçêo após 1 s: 0.78 rad (erro 0.02 rad)
assentou (erro < 0.02 rad): em 992 ms
sobressinal: 1 % · posiçêo após 1 s: 0.77 rad (erro 0.03 rad)
assentou (erro < 0.02 rad): nêo, em 1 s
sobressinal: 0 % · posiçêo após 1 s: 0.77 rad (erro 0.03 rad)
assentou (erro < 0.02 rad): nêo, em 1 s

Two lines per kd (0, 2, and 10). With no kd, the knee overshoots the step by 49% and takes almost 1 s to settle. With kd 2, it overshoots only 1%. With kd 2 or 10, the joint stops 0.03 rad short of the target: with no integral term, the PD needs an error to produce the torque that holds up the leg's weight.

03 · The G1 standing with PD alone

ficar_em_pe.py puts the robot on the ground in the default pose and holds each joint for 5 s, with no policy. Standing = pelvis between 0.70 and 0.85 m and tilt below 20°.

Terminal · kp 300, kd 5 on all of them
F=${FTH:-$HOME/fth}; cd $F/locomocao && . $F/.venv/bin/activate
python3 kit/ficar_em_pe.py --kp 300 --kd 5; echo "saída: $?"
Terminal · the policy's gains
F=${FTH:-$HOME/fth}; cd $F/locomocao && . $F/.venv/bin/activate
python3 kit/ficar_em_pe.py --ganhos rl_lab; echo "saída: $?"
Example from the repository

With kp 300 on every joint, the G1 turns into a statue: it stands up, tilted 1°, asking for at most 12 N·m.

ganhos: kp/kd por junta do unitree_rl_lab
altura da pelve: 0.79 m no início → 0.09 m após 5 s · inclinaçêo: 95°
maior torque pedido: 41 N·m · juntas que bateram no limite de torque: 2
de pé (pelve entre 0.70 e 0.85 m, inclinaçêo < 20°): nêo
saída: 1

With the per-joint gains from deploy.yaml (hip 100/2, knee 150/4, ankle 40/2, waist 200/5, arms 40/10), the robot collapses and two joints hit the torque limit. Measured here with the same kp on every joint, the standing G1 only stays up with kp from about 200.

This is not a bug: the policy was trained with these gains, and it is the policy that closes the balance loop. Changing the gains invalidates the policy.

ERRO: passe --kp e --kd (ex.: --kp 100 --kd 2) ou --ganhos rl_lab.
saída: 1

The script requires both gains or the ready-made set. PD is a pair: kp without kd is a spring with no brake.

04 · How RL repos store the PD

What changes when you read a real deploy.yaml.

1. Per joint

In the unitree_rl_lab deploy.yaml, stiffness (kp) and damping (kd) come as a list with one value per joint, in the Unitree SDK order.

On the robot, the deploy sends q, dq, kp, kd and tau for each motor every cycle; the driver runs the PD.

2. Order

The default pose and the actions come in Isaac Lab order, and joint_ids_map converts between them. Mixing the orders brings the robot down in 1 s: course 2, lesson 6, shows the mistake.

3. Fixed

The gains go into training: the policy learns to walk with that PD. On the robot, using other kp/kd means running a different policy from the one you trained.

07 · In three sentences

PDTorque = kp × error − kd × velocity, clipped at the motor limit.
MeasureWithout kd, 49% overshoot; with kd 2, 1%, and a 0.03 rad error that only the policy corrects.
RuleThe gains are part of the policy: change the gains and you change the policy.
