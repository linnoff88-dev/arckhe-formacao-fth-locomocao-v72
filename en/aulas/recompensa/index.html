Lesson 4 · 16 min

Designing the gait reward

The policy learns what the reward pays for, not what you meant. In this lesson you read the gait terms of the mjlab velocity task, record one rollout walking and another standing still, measure how much each term paid, and find out that, with one weight swapped, standing still becomes worth more than walking.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The 7 gait terms the kit copies from mjlab, with name, weight and scale
  2. 2Walking: 53.60. Standing still with the same command: 42.32
  3. 3One swapped weight: standing still (40.22) beats walking (39.65)
  4. 4How unitree_rl_lab chose different terms for the same robot

Goal: measure how much each reward term pays in a recorded rollout and predict the effect of changing a weight before you train.

01 · Rewards and penalties

Click each block. Each term is worth weight × value × 0.02 s per policy step.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

Names, weights, and std copied from the mjlab velocity task (commit 8bb2bc9, 10/18/2026). The kit uses a single std for pose.

02 · The kit's reward

A YAML file with one term per line. This is the file you will copy and change.

File · kit/recompensa.yaml
# Recompensa de marcha do kit (FTH 1). Nomes, pesos e std seguem a tarefa de velocidade do mjlab
# (src/mjlab/tasks/velocity/velocity_env_cfg.py, commit 8bb2bc9, 18/10/2026); "pose" usa um std só.
# Cada termo vale weight × valor × 0,02 s por passo da política. Positivo = prêmio; negativo = punição.
track_linear_velocity: {weight: 2.0, std: 0.5}     # exp(-erro² / std²) entre comando e velocidade xy
track_angular_velocity: {weight: 2.0, std: 0.707}  # o mesmo para a rotação em z
upright: {weight: 1.0, std: 0.447}                 # tronco em pé (gravidade sem componente x/y)
pose: {weight: 1.0, std: 0.3}                      # juntas perto da pose padrão
dof_pos_limits: {weight: -1.0}                     # quanto as juntas passaram do limite (rad)
action_rate_l2: {weight: -0.1}                     # ação que muda de um passo para o outro
foot_slip: {weight: -0.1}                          # pé que escorrega enquanto está no chão

04 · Change one weight and redo the math

A copy of the YAML with track_linear_velocity at 0.5 instead of 2.0. Nothing is trained: only the math changes.

Terminal · weight 0.5 on tracking
F=${FTH:-$HOME/fth}; cd $F/locomocao && . $F/.venv/bin/activate
sed "s/track_linear_velocity: {weight: 2.0/track_linear_velocity: {weight: 0.5/" kit/recompensa.yaml > $F/treino/minha.yaml
python3 kit/termos.py $F/treino/andando.npz --recompensa $F/treino/minha.yaml | tail -1
python3 kit/termos.py $F/treino/parado.npz --comando-vx 0.7 --recompensa $F/treino/minha.yaml | tail -1
Example from the repository
RECOMPENSA DO EPISÓDIO                           39.65
RECOMPENSA DO EPISÓDIO                           40.22

First line: walking, 39.65. Second: standing still, 40.22. With this weight, a training run that finds "stand still and upright" is rewarded for it: that is reward hacking before any training. Doing this math takes seconds; finding out after a training run costs hours of GPU.

05 · Same robot, different recipe

unitree_rl_lab (commit 4960b84) trains the same G1 with different terms. Click to compare.

1. Alive

alive with weight 0.15: a small reward for each step spent upright. Lesson 6 shows what happens when it is missing and when it is too large.

2. Height

base_height with weight −10 and a target of 0.78 m: penalizes the pelvis being off height. It keeps the robot from learning to walk crouched or on its knees.

3. Tilt

flat_orientation_l2 with weight −5 penalizes a tilted torso, the role upright plays in mjlab.

4. Gait

gait with weight 0.5 rewards the stepping pattern: a period of 0.8 s, with the two feet half a period out of phase (alternating). Without a term like this, the policy may find a strange way to get around, like dragging its feet.

06 · Practice

Check the reward before spending a training run.

E1 · Term by term

5 min

Record both rollouts and run termos.py on the walking rollout.

Done when: python3 kit/termos.py $F/treino/andando.npz; echo "saída: $?" → RECOMPENSA DO EPISÓDIO … 53.60 and saída: 0.

E2 · Standing still

5 min

Run the standing-still rollout charged as if it should walk, and work out how much it is worth compared to walking.

Done when: the output ends in 42.32 and you wrote "standing still is worth 79% of walking".

E3 · The weight that flips the game

10 min

Run the block with a weight of 0.5. Then, in your own copy of the YAML, find the lowest track_linear_velocity weight at which walking is still worth more than standing still.

Done when: with 0.5, the second line (40.22) is greater than the first (39.65); you noted the weight at which walking starts winning again (on 10/19/2026: 0.55 still loses, 0.6 wins).

08 · In three sentences

SumThe reward is a sum of weighted terms; each term pays or punishes at every step.
MeasureWalking 53.60, standing still 42.32; with tracking at 0.5, standing still starts to win.
HabitBefore training, measure the reward of a good rollout and a bad one.
