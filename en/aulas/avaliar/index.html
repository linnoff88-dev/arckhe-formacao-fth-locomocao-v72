Lesson 7 · 16 min

Evaluating for real: speed, turning and push

A policy is only ready when it passes a test you wrote before looking at the result. In this lesson you evaluate the ready-made policy over 20 episodes with fixed seeds: speed error, turning and pushes of 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 m/s. Then you run your checkpoint from lesson 5 through the same test.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Speed error: 0.05 m/s between requested and measured
  2. 2Push of 1.0 m/s: 20/20 standing; 1.5: 16/20; 2.0: 9/20
  3. 3Fixed seeds: the same 20 episodes for any policy
  4. 4Where evaluation in sim ends and sim2sim begins (course 2)

Goal: evaluate a policy over 20 episodes with fixed seeds, measuring speed error and survival to pushes of increasing strength.

01 · What a walking test measures

Click each block. avaliar.py runs all four in every episode.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

The test is written before the result: number of episodes, command range, strength, and pass criterion (e.g., 18/20).

02 · The ready-made policy in the test

20 episodes of 10 s, push of 1.0 m/s. Exits 0 when all survive.

Terminal · evaluate with a push
F=${FTH:-$HOME/fth}; cd $F/locomocao && . $F/.venv/bin/activate
python3 kit/avaliar.py --episodios 20 --empurrao 1.0; echo "saída: $?"
Terminal · stronger pushes
F=${FTH:-$HOME/fth}; cd $F/locomocao && . $F/.venv/bin/activate
for e in 1.5 2.0; do python3 kit/avaliar.py --episodios 20 --empurrao $e | tail -1; done
Example from the repository
política: unitree_rl_lab velocity v0 (ONNX) · 20 episódios de 10 s · giro +0.0 rad/s · empurrão de 1.0 m/s aos 4 s
erro médio de velocidade (quem não caiu): 0.05 m/s
sobreviveu: 20/20
saída: 0

20 out of 20 stayed up with a 1.0 m/s push, above the training (up to 0.5 m/s per axis, about 0.71 m/s on the diagonal), with an average velocity error of 0.05 m/s.

sobreviveu: 16/20
sobreviveu: 9/20

At 1.5 m/s, 16 out of 20; at 2.0 m/s, 9 out of 20. The margin runs out between 1.0 and 1.5 m/s. This kind of curve, not a single number, tells you how much the policy can take.

03 · Your checkpoint in the same test

avaliar.py accepts a .pt trained by the kit in place of the ready-made policy. Same seeds.

Terminal · your policy
F=${FTH:-$HOME/fth}; cd $F/locomocao && . $F/.venv/bin/activate
python3 kit/avaliar.py --politica $F/treino/ppo/modelo_20.pt --episodios 5 | tail -2
Example from the repository
quedas: ep 0 em 1.3 s, ep 1 em 1.4 s, ep 2 em 1.4 s, ep 3 em 1.4 s, ep 4 em 1.3 s
sobreviveu: 0/5

0 out of 5, all falls before 1.5 s, even without a push. That is expected for 20 iterations: the test confirms what the curves already said. The value matters less than the habit: every policy, ready-made or yours, goes through the same test.

04 · How far simulation evaluation goes

What this test proves and what is left for course 2.

1. Proves

That the policy tracks the command and withstands pushes in MuJoCo, with the Menagerie G1. The ready-made policy was trained in Isaac Lab: passing here is already a first sign of robustness.

2. Does not prove

That it walks on the robot. Different delay, actuator, friction, and mass are the subject of course 2, which measures this gap and runs the policy through a sim2sim gate with DDS before it touches the floor.

3. Concept

The concept of evaluating in simulation and of the reality gap, for manipulation policies, is in vla-v72, lesson 6.

5 min
